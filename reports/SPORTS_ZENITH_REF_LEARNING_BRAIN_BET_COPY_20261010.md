## Known limitations
- WebAudio whistle is intentionally short/quiet and user-triggered, but its ability to produce actual sound is device/mute/browser dependent. No prerecorded copyright media asset, microphone, push notification, or server-side sound file is needed.
- The Ref uses CSS pose/flag movement and can switch dock side; it is **not** a full 3D animated body, draggable across any viewport coordinate, or a real league official.
- Betting fair price derives from **market** consensus and has no independently proven winning model edge; no "BET ON" side means a validated recommendation. All bidirectional prices remain research-only.
- The original real external Knockout Pool requires selecting and sending a Week 5 pick to Scott and receiving an “OK” acknowledgement; website changes are not submissions. Verified season deadline remains Saturday Oct10 6 PM Eastern.
