# Documented fees versus saved NFL prices

Fourteen saved pre-kickoff outcome asks were checked against the frozen model, documented taker fee formulas and captured per-market settings. Five raw probability/price gaps were positive; after documented entry fees only two conditional modeled returns remain positive, none reaches 3%, and one cent of worse fill price removes every positive case. Best conditional modeled return is 1.2811%. These are counterfactual expected returns, not realized ROI or current offers.

Kalshi source: https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf, effective July7 2026, read through the web retrieval tool. General taker coefficient is .07 and the KXNFLGAME row lists maker/taker multipliers1. Captured series metadata agrees: quadratic_with_maker_fees, multiplier1. Captured event-change pages are complete. Formula math uses https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/fee_rounding: a single buy fill, ceil6decimal fee and the conservative non-direct-member cent balance precision, with no rebate credited. Polymarket sources: https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees and https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/market-details. The general Sports rate is.05, but the official market-details guidance identifies feeSchedule as the individual market setting. Saved validated NFL settings rate.03/exponent1/takerOnly true were used, at five-decimal fee precision. We do not substitute the general category rate or convert legacy base_fee1000 into an equivalent fee. Actual execution fee reconciliation remains pending.

Every sensitivity assumes100 contracts filled once at the observed ask and held to settlement. The amount includes entry fee in acquisition capital. One-cent worse-fill stress recalculates the price-dependent fee. Separate1% tie-probability sensitivity changes payout expectation to (1-tie)*conditional_win_probability + .5*tie; this is an assumption, not a fitted tie projection. Observed depth is reported but does not guarantee a100-contract fill. Funding costs, rebates, actual fills and cancellation-adjusted probabilities are unverified; no returns from them are credited.

Four fee/ROI tests pass. The existing net-ROI panel shows the conditional result with its original price-capture time and preserves realized ROI as unavailable. No trade, paper entry, new model, new schedule or approval was created. Raw receipts and source hashes are preserved in nfl_live/prediction_markets/nfl_fee_sensitivity_latest.json.
