# ROI is the primary objective

The narrowed NFL winner research now leads with net ROI, net profit and maximum drawdown. Accuracy metrics remain model diagnostics and cannot substitute for verified profitable returns. No minimum overall win rate or global Brier gain is itself required to demonstrate a selective ROI advantage. Strategy validation requires a frozen selection/sizing rule, forward execution/cost assumptions, complete grading and sufficient evidence of repeatability.

Accounting uses actual or simulated fill price times quantity plus entry fee as capital committed. Net profit is settlement payout minus acquisition cost and exit fees. ROI is aggregate net profit divided by aggregate settled acquisition capital including entry fees. Spread and slippage belong in fill price; subtracting them a second time would double-count. Maximum drawdown is the decline from the running peak of cumulative settled net profit in dollars; this is not mark-to-market portfolio drawdown. The reporting separates open positions from settled ROI and refuses mixed actual/paper modes, duplicate identities, invalid amounts or settled rows with unverified costs/settlement. Costs or settlement unknown means profit and ROI unavailable. No settled trades means unavailable, not zero ROI.
