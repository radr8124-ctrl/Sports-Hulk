## User-facing PDF workflow
- Existing `src/SurvivorPoolImport.jsx` now renders a compact, beautiful retro-modern **KNOCKOUT POOL · OFFICIAL FILES / Upload weekly PDF** row; native disclosure stays collapsed until tapped, accepts iPhone Files PDFs, also XLSX/CSV. Its original **Preview pool file**, complete ticket count, weekly picks, duplicate ticket count, sample entries, separate I authorize confirmation and **Confirm pool import** remain intact. It says scanned PDFs may require text-searchable originals and reported losses alone are NOT a complete organizer sheet.
- The one existing import panel stays **after the personal protected pick editor** to respect pick-first information architecture; a separate **Upload weekly PDF** quick action near the top (under the deadline/access card) scrolls to and opens it. The quick action is rendered ONLY for `saved.pool_manager_enabled===true`, returned on already-authenticated private state from the original server-side allowlist. The real import endpoint still performs fresh JWT and manager role checks on every preview/commit; the UI flag has no authority.
- `commercial_web/survivor_pool_bridge.py` adds a **read-only exact-entry preview** limited to names from the authenticated server's actual linked account. For each owner ticket it shows `Found your entry` or `Needs manual review`, the specific team(s) for the PDF's current week, and **separate same-name ticket** count. E.g. a synthetic Week 5 organizer sheet can preview "Houston Texans" for SYNTHETIC QA 01. This does NOT cause a Texans pick to be saved, backdated, submitted or certified by the organizer. Extra or malformed same-name tickets NEVER silently merge into another entry.
- The preview RPC `focus_entry_names` is populated only server-side via `linkedSurvivorEntries(req,user.id)` after the authenticated manager check, never copied from a browser-supplied entry name or query. Original whole-pool import, file version rules, private entry/past-history protection and reviewer confirmation are unchanged.
