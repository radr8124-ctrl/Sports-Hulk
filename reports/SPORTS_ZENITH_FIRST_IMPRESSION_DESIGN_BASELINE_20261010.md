## Non-cheesy welcome-screen design integration contract
- This future entrance is **the first impression**: capture attention and communicate value in under 3 seconds. It must make the visitor want to **enter the real Sports Zenith product**, not make them endure a separate cinematic introduction. The existing homepage itself already has an assertive headline and visual hierarchy; preserve and **elevate** that instead of introducing unfamiliar dark-space / stadium-spotlight visuals.
- Treat current real typography, cream/navy cards, thick dark borders, striped coral/gold/aqua accent, muted cream background, and confident but playful dashboard style as the **approved reference language**. The future welcome must feel like an expertly edited opening cover in the same magazine. Avoid loose gaming/casino/sports slogans, stock stadium blasts, giant spinning helmets, neon clutter or AI spectacle.
- Draft mobile structure: compact real Sports Zenith brand; short brand promise (final copy owner-approved); restrained broadcast-data motif; ONE clear prominent **Explore Sports Zenith** guest entry; distinct **Sign In** using existing email-code modal. Desktop may reveal more content but must keep main entry obvious. First CTA and Sign In visible without scrolling at 320/390px. The intro must never force an account for public pages or break direct links and returning users.
- Match both captured real homepage screenshots side by side when prototyping. Record whether the entrance actually drives people into content: guest Explore action, sign-in open/completion rate and immediate bounce (opt-in privacy-safe aggregate signals only; do not imply these metrics currently exist). It must feel intriguing/credible, not shouty. Prefer shorter text, short motion and fast load over decorative complexity.
- Keep the secure existing passwordless login and current live homepage untouched until core Survivor review and remaining proof checks are complete. This is a design reference and requirement, **not** a deployed opening screen or an agreed final hero image.

## Evidence limitations
Production screenshots are Chromium responsive references, not native iPhone Safari measurements. No new page or hero variant was rendered or approved in this batch; owner approval, responsive and authentication testing are future acceptance steps. The public homepage was not modified.
