# Sports Zenith — Safe Week 5 → Week 6 Survivor Rollover and Owner Readiness

Date: October 10, 2026 ET. Existing private Sports Zenith Survivor; no separate app, no new user selection, no official organizer submission and no change to original pool records.

## Original risk found before this patch
- At approximately 6:41 PM EDT the actual protected `ANNIE G 01` record was `pool_current_week=5`, `status=ALIVE`, five original recorded wins across Weeks 1–4 (Chargers, 49ers, Chiefs, Bills, Vikings), `week_5.picks=[]`, `current_picks=[]`, `week_5.entry_result=OPEN` and `week_5.submitted=false`. The original organizer import `LATEST_OFFICIAL_POOL.json` still certified only **Week 4**, 364 numbered entries. Reported Dallas Week 5 losses were a separate unverified claim and are not official pool ticket evidence.
- The existing `nfl_live/update_survivor_current_week.py` determined season week using schedule/strategy and called `open_current_week` for ALIVE entries whenever the model week increased, permitting a prior week with `entry_result=OPEN` (or no result) to advance into a new round. This could automatically reset current picks and open Week 6 as though the organizer had certified Week 5 survival. This is dangerous specifically for an ALIVE prior-round record without the organizer's current pool sheet or verified completed entry. The original source may refresh continuously, but source freshness is not ownership/result proof.

## New protected writer guard
- `nfl_live/update_survivor_current_week.py` now exports pure `can_advance(entry,from_week,to_week)` and `can_advance_pool(data,target_week)` gates. To advance from Week 5 to Week 6 the entry MUST be ALIVE in the exact prior week, have an `official_pool_sheet_confirmed` TRUE original field (rule-status text alone does not suffice), original 2026 rule count exactly 1 in Week 5 (2 for Week 3 etc), a prior-week `entry_result=WIN`, the exact complete unique team-pick set and every pick terminal WIN. No duplicate, pending, lost, unrecorded, rule-conflicted or jumped weeks qualify. Already-eliminated entries do not reopen automatically. In Week 3 double pick, both distinct winners are required.
- A globally suggested candidate week cannot advance if **any active ticket** is not certified. If the source would jump backward, skip a week, or move into Week 6 while the last official Week 5 source/result is still incomplete, it prints `ROLLOVER_HELD_AWAITING_ORGANIZER_WEEK_RESULT` and RETURNS **before writing a byte** to the original private `SURVIVOR_ENTRIES.json`. Only a full official prior-week win allows normal opening of the next round. Existing ordinary same-week refresh operations remain supported.
- Direct Python script invocation from the existing postrefresh shell wrapper now correctly inserts the absolute existing repository into `sys.path` before importing the authoritative `nfl_live.survivor_pool_rules` rule implementation. This behavior was explicitly tested without invoking the real writer.
- This guard is NOT permission to fabricate a Week 5 pool ticket, assign a team or certify an external submission. The original grader's independent ESPN-vs-NFLverse Final proof should be hardened separately before any automated official settlement of a newly imported ticket is promoted; this batch's protective gate is about **not allowing an incomplete prior week to roll**.
