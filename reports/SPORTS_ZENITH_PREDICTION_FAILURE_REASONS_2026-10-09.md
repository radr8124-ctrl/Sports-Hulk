# Sports Zenith — Why some game and prop predictions are so poor

**2026-10-09 UTC · Source-verified retrospective root-cause audit. No changes to live betting, probability models or existing results.**

## Core diagnosis

**1. Sportsbook mirroring disguised as game-line model skill.** From 186 settled original V2 all-markets game-line price observations, exactly **186/186** model probabilities are identical to the corresponding sportsbook market-reference probabilities. **182** entries have `MARKET_REFERENCE_INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`; only **4** are plain `MARKET_REFERENCE`. Original research gates correctly returned **180 PASS_NO_PROVEN_INDEPENDENT_LANE_EDGE** and **6 PASS_CONTRADICTORY_GAME_SIDES**; **none were approved as independently proven bets**. This game-line cohort does *not* show a learned model adding information beyond the bookmaker; a 61.8% line hit rate or +4.98% simulated paper ROI cannot demonstrate it beats the market.

**2. NBA spread failures were real but clustered, not 39 independent games.** The 2026–27 NBA **preseason** source recorded **10W–29L from 39 alternate spread lines in just eight official games**, **−55.02% original price-proxy paper ROI**. All 39 original grades were cross-checked against independent ESPN final scores; no actual source result mismatch was found. A strictly retrospective first-recorded spread per game sensitivity was **3W–5L / −31.63% source paper ROI**, showing losses remain but are less exaggerated than the quote-weight raw result. The model probability for *every* spread record was exactly the sportsbook reference, and every original research recommendation was PASS. This is NOT proof a model predicting the spread independently is bad; rather there was no demonstrated independent model at all for those lines.

**3. NBA totals looked better because overlapping predictions and bad clock identities inflated the evidence.** Original totals 84W–22L on 106 lines from 20 preseason games. Independent crosswalk found **8 duplicate old/new regime market-side lines** and **7 lines with original recorded event start 15 minutes earlier than ESPN's official tip**. After strict clock validation and first original quote per exact game/market/side/line: **91 distinct lines over 19 games, 69W–22L**, +26.88% priced paper ROI, 95% game-cluster interval −7.97% to +54.40%, and **model Brier = sportsbook Brier**. Replacing only this NBA totals subgroup in the uncorrected pooled game scorecard changes the *retrospective sensitivity* from +4.98% paper ROI to −0.69%. This is **not a new official full-ledger performance calculation**; all other sports still need their own audit. No historical source was rewritten.

**4. NHL independently adjusted props have a genuine calibration problem.** Exactly **799** NHL_PROP records came from the `LANE_CORE_MODEL` in **16 independent game blocks**. Its mean model-to-market probability adjustment was **7.214 percentage points in absolute value**. Paired Brier (lower is better) was **0.224868 model vs 0.211297 bookmaker reference** (+0.013571 worse). By original market subtype: assists 0.213947 vs 0.199979 (295 correlated player lines), goals 0.210722 vs 0.205015 (218), points 0.236573 vs 0.211230 (163), and shots 0.260620 vs 0.249663 (123). In the 70–80% model confidence band, outcomes won 68.55% against mean predicted 75.07% (159 correlated lines); 80%+ predictions won 75.58% vs predicted 84.28% (86 lines). These point to overconfidence in this sample but MUST be evaluated by independent games, not player-leg count.

The separately stored game-cluster bootstrap of the NHL model-minus-bookmaker Brier averaged **+0.018340** with 95% source-game resampling interval **[+0.006759, +0.032415]** across only 16 games, and remains +0.012759 when the worst block is omitted. This supports the observation that these historical adjustments hurt calibration, not a claim they will inevitably underperform in new seasons. Source receipt: `reports/NHL_ADJUSTED_PROP_GAME_CLUSTER_BRIER_AUDIT_2026-10-09.json`.

**5. High win rate without favorable quoted odds is not profitable.** Original 988 graded `MLB_PROP` lines (nine independent official games) were all **market-reference probabilities**, not a new learned model, and went **693W–295L = 70.1%** but **−0.94% paper priced ROI**. Prices/fees and selection matter. A betting model cannot be approved because it correctly identifies heavy favorites while paying too little per win.

## Hugging Face verification and limitations

Hugging Face authenticated and public dataset metadata checked:
- [2025–26 NBA closing lines](https://huggingface.co/datasets/minmaxxxer/nba-closing-lines-ats-2025-26) and [2026 MLB player-prop prices](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SmartStake/mlb-player-props).
- The previously locked 2025–26 NBA historical benchmark uniquely matched **1,214 regular-season official games**, with **503 held-out games**: the added-feature NBA TOTAL model scored Brier **0.254588**, worse than sportsbook-close Brier **0.250004**. NBA SPREAD features scored **0.248672** vs book **0.249909**, a tiny potential improvement with an uncertainty interval crossing zero; **no proven edge**. This is historical, not the current unseen 2026–27 season. Bookmaker original executable prices, source quote timestamps and downstream commercial licensing are NOT independently verified.
- Hugging Face supports dataset sourcing, evaluations, versions and experiment provenance; it cannot turn raw odds or sports data into an automatic winning model. **No paid cloud job or new training was run or paid for.**

## What we should fix, in priority order

1. **Separate bookmaker baseline from independent model projections.** A `MARKET_REFERENCE` probability cannot be advertised as evidence Zenith learned a winning signal. Keep all PASS decisions visible and do not make them commercial bets.
2. **Make the single-source-truth game identity gate mandatory:** exact ESPN/official start, home/away IDs, original sportsbook/provider event and quote timestamps. Quarantine mismatches before grading or pricing. Separate preseason, regular season and playoffs for both presentation and validation.
3. **Use independently frozen ONE prediction/line per event for headline ROI.** Keep all alternate lines in the source ledger for forensic history but report correlated variants only as secondary research. Never silently delete wins or losses.
4. **NHL adjustments become a separate challenger, not an edge claim.** On newly frozen games compare raw bookmaker baseline, frozen current adjusted model and a pre-registered conservative shrinkage variant. Score proper Brier/log-loss and same-price paper EV, bootstrap by game and calendar time. Require enough games and verified prices before recommending any promotion.
5. **Use injury, starter, official lineups, travel/rest, roster changes only if source-verified AS OF forecast time.** Those may be candidate explanations for errors, not established causes in this audit. Avoid features taken from after the game, stale player IDs and wrongly assumed goalie starters.
6. **Prove priced value and abstain otherwise.** User-facing confidence must not outrun favorable offered odds, data quality, independent sample size, and current original book quote. No forced 70% picks.

**Bottom line:** The current weakness is not merely 'pick better teams'. The original game-line 'brain' often replicated book probabilities without adding skill, the NHL feature adjustments were less accurate than the book in this sample, the apparent NBA totals profitability was partly affected by source duplicates/timestamps, and high hit rates did not establish viable returns. No profitable or consistent 70%-hit edge has been verified.
