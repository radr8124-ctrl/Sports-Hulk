# Sports Zenith — Outcome Learning V1: Accurate Error Autopsies and Controlled Shadow Corrections
**Established Oct 10, 2026, UTC** | **Code version:** ZENITH_ASOF_MARKET_RESIDUAL_SHADOW_V1

## User objective
The model must get smarter from its wrong predictions: understand *what* failed, investigate *why*, propose/test a change, and learn reliably across independent future games. It must never rewrite previously frozen forecasts, learn from results not available at the original prediction time, pretend a market estimate is its own model, or automatically convert research into a bet.

## Actual production implementation
- A new source-only feedback engine: `intelligence_warehouse/outcome_learning/learning_core.py` and `build_outcome_learning.py`, with independent unit and browser regression tests.
- Replays original append-only `BETTING_V2_ALL_MARKETS_FORWARD_LEDGER.jsonl` ENTRY + SETTLED events rather than retroactively changing frozen predictions. Quarantines conflicting entry/settlement, invalid before/after-kickoff clocks, implausible probabilities and grades inconsistent with recorded final score, and never converts missing results into losses.
- The original `grade_snapshot_at` timestamp is a historical recommendation timestamp, *not* independent evidence that a result was available before kickoff. The actual source event settlement time is used as the conservative as-of training clock. No official per-game box-score double-check for the large cross-sport game ledger has been claimed. The original freeze, source SHA, and result availability clocks are kept separate.
- Diagnoses outcomes by explicit type: WRONG OUTRIGHT WINNER; WINNER WON BUT DID NOT COVER; SPREAD MARGIN MISS; UNDERDOG LOST OUTSIDE SPREAD; TOTAL ABOVE/BELOW LINE. These are factual results, not unsupported claims that a particular injury, coach decision or weather change caused the error.
- Actual Oct 4 Chiefs 30–Raiders 27 game case source: original `nfl_live/decision/history/NFL_GRADED_RECOMMENDATIONS.csv`, ESPN event 401872976, frozen ~19:27 UTC before 20:25 UTC kickoff. Chiefs moneyline WON; Chiefs -5 SPREAD LOST because KC only won by 3; OVER 47.5 WON with 57 total. The saved 95/93/89 research scores are NOT calibrated winning probabilities and are not evidence of 2/3 profitable bets. All three original grades require consistent home/away final score proof. The Oct 4 research result is not passed off as the later frozen nine-feature NFL model.
- Audited current game-market forward record: first 841 frozen entries; 197 settled market research outcomes, 51 independent game blocks; 126 WIN / 71 LOSS, 0 pushes. All 197 model probability fields are exactly equal to the market reference probability, i.e. NO independent model edge. Missing result source event identity for 75 rows prevents attribution to a verified independent source. 133 historical `grade_snapshot_at` fields refer to a pregame recommendation snapshot (not necessarily a settlement-source mistake). No opponent, injury or weather feature is automatically blamed on a wrong result.
- The largest lane, NBA_TOTAL, has 111 graded research rows across only 21 independent games, not 111 independent examples. Per-lane Brier scores are calculated after equal game-block averaging, not just counting alternate total quotes as independent bets.
- Explicit hypotheses per lane: moneyline probabilistic calibration, spread margin/cover modeling separately from game winner, totals pace/scoring variance. Causal assertions are not permitted from a single game.

## Real shadow model training and promotion gates
- Pre-registered calibration-only residual learner is available in `learning_core.shadow_calibration`. It may be **frozen for a NEW future prediction** only when at least 30 independent same sport+market+competition regime games, at least 50 original valid outcome rows, and at least 10 independent games in the same probability band were settled **before the original future prediction capture time**. Same game, future/post-start labels, different NBA preseason/regular/unknown groups, unsupported or unavailable result-source IDs are excluded.
- Current status: **0 qualified prospective shadow forecasts** from currently pending bets; histories remain insufficient after applying as-of eligibility, even though 197 prior observations exist. This is an honest maturity gate, not a software failure or guarantee that tomorrow's model will outperform.
- A conservative residual adjustment is capped at +/-5 percentage points and never displayed as a validated independent model. A separate append-only `FROZEN_CALIBRATION_SHADOW.jsonl` is prepared and will be written only if the maturity and pregame conditions are met.
- After at least 50 settled shadow predictions and 30 independent held-out games, a **strictly positive 90% game-block Brier-advantage lower bound** permits a MANUAL MODEL REVIEW only. Independent ROI, CLV and price availability are separate requirements. At no stage does the script automatically change production weights, offer wagers, publish winner confidence as proven, or spend real bankroll.
