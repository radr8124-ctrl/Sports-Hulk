# Sports Zenith — PolyPredictr Product Reference Review
**Observed live:** 2026-10-09 UTC
**Website:** https://polypredictr.com
**Purpose:** Keep useful third-party design intelligence; no UI code copied, no claim of model edge.

## What was actually tested
Opened the public homepage, /browse, /faq, /posts, and /copy-trade with mobile Chromium. Selected Sports market category, clicked a sports-related market, and opened its Signal Lab screen without signing in. The public marketing site displays live/refreshing market counts and summaries which vary by capture and are not authoritative proof of backend correctness.

## Product and UX pattern
- The site is for Polymarket and Kalshi prediction-market contracts, **not** full sportsbook NFL/MLB/NHL/NBA odds/props.
- Homepage emphasizes one simple flow: Browse market, Read AI edge, Act with context. Free pick cards surface a **Poly Score**, **Confidence**, **Payout multiplier**, a yes/no side, and a short explanation. Long-form analysis is gated.
- Mobile dark theme with compact filterable market listings (all, trending, highest pay, bookmarked), category chips incl. Sports, venue filter, sorting by volume, prominent one-tap Signal Lab details.
- Signal Lab observed free sports card: Poly Score 64; Confidence 95%; 1.06x payout; the page's explanation explicitly says the underlying market assigns about 95% probability to the selected NO side. That public demonstration does **not** prove independently calibrated AI forecast skill. FAQ claims confidence comes from data convergence and Poly Score blends confidence and payout, but no verifiable calibration or holdout analysis visible.
- FAQ describes reference-class forecasting plus volume, sentiment, market movement and cross-market correlation and labels deep research as the larger workspace. No underlying model source/ledger visible.
- Landing page advertises seven-day price trajectories, named risks, hourly research, and public resolved-pick results. /posts displayed a single welcome announcement during inspection; a source-linked public granular prediction ledger was not located. /copy-trade showed a leaderboard with large P/L figures but no audited individual trades, identities, or settlement proof visible in the inspected UI.
- A public /browse notice said **Polymarket works; Kalshi and other platforms coming soon**, while the same public screen displayed Kalshi records and the marketing advertised both integrated. Treat support/recency labels as potentially inconsistent until corroborated.

## Relevance for Sports Zenith — not copy-paste
- Adopt the **information hierarchy**, not the unverified numeric confidence: one concise outcome card with the **exact selection** and sportsbook/odds or pick-em MORE/LESS, **market no-vig probability**, **genuine separate model probability if one exists**, comparison and actual source age, then short reasons/risks and expandable technical proof.
- Separate three measures: (1) bookmaker market fair probability, (2) model estimate with backtest/forward qualification, (3) bet/entry expected value at *specific book* and payout. Never label market probability "AI confidence" or imply better-to-median bookmaker price equals positive EV.
- Permit research watchlists and explicit PASS/NOT PROVEN statuses without making the entire site display only PASS. Keep a truthful frozen prospective ledger with source identity and immutable outcomes. Do not imitate unaudited leaderboards or automatic copy-trading.
- Helpful future enhancements if time/data permit: timestamped line movement with comparable same-line snapshots, named catalyst/injury check tied to source time, reviewable trail of projected probabilities vs market and actual results, and optional prefilled question to Ask for the exact bet.
- Existing Sports Zenith protection before this third-party review: odds-first game display; exact home/away and total settlement descriptions; NFL original market-assisted model reference; NFL four-week prop research matched to the exact player/line; mobile tab scroll correction. Those were separate site fixes already underway; no PolyPredictr model or source code used.

**Limitation:** No authenticated PolyPredictr account was used; paid Deep Analysis and underlying accuracy evidence were not accessible, so no claim of performance equivalence, superiority or independently tested AI edge.
