# Sports Zenith — final-launch cinematic welcome and sign-in experience

Status: APPROVED AS A FUTURE FEATURE REQUEST, NOT YET DESIGNED OR DEPLOYED
Request date: October 10, 2026
Source: Owner wants a really cool sign-in page that feels like a premium opening page for the website, AFTER core Sports Zenith features are complete.

## Creative direction to prototype for owner approval
A premium, cinematic **Enter Sports Zenith** welcome experience: the feeling of entering a stadium or sports broadcast command center, not a generic login form or a gaudy gambling ad. Brand-forward SPORTS ZENITH wordmark, dramatic but clean stadium-light glow or scoreboard/grid accents, deep ink navy with aqua and tasteful coral/gold details matching the existing premium site. A headline with the idea of sports intelligence, a very short explanation of Scores / Survivor / Fantasy / Bets, and one exceptionally clear entrance action. Offer a clearly distinct **Sign In / Join** action. On larger displays, cinematic arena imagery/animation can surround the main message; on iPhone, the page must feel like a beautiful single-screen entry, with concise text and accessible targets.

## Product safeguards
- **Build after** the pending Survivor owner review and core source-proof work; do not interrupt current production sign-in during Week 5 review.
- **Reuse** the existing secure email one-time-code authentication and member-linked Survivor/DFS/fantasy storage. No separate login database, no password storage and no hidden account-claim bypass.
- Support returning member access, account creation using the same verified email code, and access to public previews where appropriate. Do not make an arbitrary gate that breaks public Scores/News/SEO.
- Preserve direct links to `/` and `#survivor` and the existing navigation/entry state; signed-in visitors may go straight into the site rather than being forced through an animation every time.
- Respect reduced-motion/accessibility and real iPhone performance. Fast first paint, compressed media, clear keyboard focus, readable contrast, no auto-playing audio, and a quick skip/enter path. Do not make the site slower to obtain a flashy video.
- Any displayed scores, market lines, injury alerts or performance stats must come from currently verified data; do not put fake live odds, wins or model accuracy on the opening page.
- Prototype and review with owner FIRST. Verify mobile and desktop, sign-in email code, deep-link return, guest route, private-account access, accessibility and fallback before production release.

## Not included in this checkpoint
No actual splash art, intro animation, login or homepage code has been created or changed. This is a durable, versioned final-launch requirement only. Owner can choose final visual direction after seeing prototypes.
