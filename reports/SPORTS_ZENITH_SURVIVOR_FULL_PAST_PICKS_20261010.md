# Sports Zenith — Entire authenticated Survivor past-picks history

Date: 2026-10-10 EDT. User report: after their Survivor account was successfully automatically linked, **"It's not showing me past picks."** Existing production Sports Zenith app; read-only change to past-pick display.

## Confirmed root cause
- Protected original `nfl_live/derived/SURVIVOR_ENTRIES.json` contains five previously settled entries for ANNIE G 01 in its `history` array: Week 1 Chargers, Week 2 49ers, Week 3 Chiefs and Bills (two picks), Week 4 Vikings. All recorded `SURVIVED`. Original used_teams includes exactly the same five distinct teams. Week 3 and Week 4 additionally have original verified terminal week_N grade and game status; Week 1 and Week 2 have original week-2 organizer PDF result provenance. Week 5 remains ALIVE/open with 0 saved picks.
- Existing `survivorPersonalScoreCards(entry,poolWeek,scores)` intentionally presents only the **latest 2 populated weekly records**, which gave Week 4 and Week 3, leaving Weeks 1 and 2 inaccessible via this view. In the existing linked-entry card, `buildLinkedSurvivorSummaries` exposed only the **latest completed week**, not an all-weeks timeline. It was a display/API coverage gap, not missing user records. No new or reconstructed historical team was necessary.

## Scoped fix
- New side-effect-free `commercial_web/survivor_past_picks.js` builds ALL recorded historical weeks strictly from `entry.history` on a previously authenticated private entry, grouping by original week and preserving two separately numbered Week 3 picks. Independently compares original `used_teams` and, where available, original week_N saved pick grades. Duplicate entries, conflicting used-team history or mismatched grades trigger `HISTORY_CONFLICT_NEEDS_REVIEW` rather than claiming an unverified win. Never creates missing weeks from the unordered burned-teams array. Current and future placeholders are excluded. Details retain original source document names and actual saved final score text only where present. No live-score feed or bookmaker probability can grade an old pool pick.
- Protected existing `GET /api/survivor/state` now includes **`past_picks`** only in the `READY` response AFTER a verified authenticated owner + linked-entry authorization. Anonymous API remains 401 and a user cannot query someone else's private history. `past_picks.total_picks=5`, `total_weeks=4`, `week_groups` 1–4 from original source, `current_picks_saved=0`, no externally executed pool submissions. Existing `pick_scores` (live/recent scoreboard) and model signals stay unchanged.
- New `src/SurvivorPastPicks.jsx`: a premium compact cream/navy **My Past Picks** historical timeline appears on the actual authenticated Survivor page immediately below access and deadline notices, **BEFORE** the Week 5 pick editor and long research lists. Week 1 Chargers survived, Week 2 49ers survived, Week 3 Chiefs and Bills survived, Week 4 Vikings survived. The header reads 5 picks / 4 weeks; Week 5 explicitly says **No pick saved in Zenith**. Full source and submission notes are tucked into an optional disclosure to minimize iPhone scrolling. Where original Week 3/4 official pool sheet indicates confirmed, this is distinguished from Zenith's per-week submitted flag; no false external organizer submission guarantee is asserted.
- Augmented only existing synthetic, localhost-only owner QA harness with five sample historical picks; no actual private account, email address, OTP, or original member data is embedded in browser test fixtures. No user action, team selections or pool import is performed.
